Harvest-Proof Time Capsule

Write a letter. Pick a date. Seal it with one hybrid-signed transaction on the QoreChain Diana testnet. It only opens on or after that date.

Testnet only, sample text only. The chain stores only the SHA-256 hash of your letter, never the letter itself, so keep your letter safe until opening day. The seal is quantum-safe because the transaction carries both a classical signature and an ML-DSA-87 post-quantum signature. The date lock is enforced by the contract, not by encryption.
