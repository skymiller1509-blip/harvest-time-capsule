# AI usage, Harvest-Proof Time Capsule

Written for the Best AI-Native Build special award ($500). Honest record of what the AI did and what the human did.

## What the AI (Mabel, a coding agent) did

- Read the live QoreChain fest site, rules, start guide, kits page and docs, and the `create-qorechain-dapp` starter, and wrote the research notes (RESEARCH.md).
- Reused the proven patterns from the Q-Day Coin Flip scaffold in `../qday-coin-flip/` (EVM chain 9800, testnet RPC, QoreX window first, hybrid proof by key registration plus EVM window, artifact generation with solc 0.8.24).
- Wrote the fresh `TimeCapsule` contract, the deploy script, the frontend, the README and the build checklist.
- Compiled the contract with solc 0.8.24 and generated the artifact file.
- Computed the real function selectors and event topics for the frontend with `cast`, instead of guessing them.
- Wrote the sample letter text used in the demo (sample text only, nothing personal).

## What the human (Eddie) did

- Chose to enter the fest and picked the Quantum Pass category.
- Picked the idea: Harvest-Proof Time Capsule, reusing his own Time-Locked Gifts concept from NEAR as inspiration.
- Owns every account the entry needs: GitHub, X, and the QoreX wallet.
- Registers for the fest, creates the public repo, connects QoreX, does the first seal on his phone, records the demo video, and completes the project on the fest site at the deadline.
- Makes every yes/no decision: which idea, whether to enter the Q-Day Drill, whether to submit.

## What the AI did not do

- No account was created by the AI, nothing was deployed, no transaction was sent, no money was spent. The scaffold was prepared only; the build, deploy and submission are still to come, with the human's yes at each step.
- No NEAR code was copied. The TimeCapsule contract is fresh Solidity written for QoreChain. The Time-Locked Gifts concept is credited in the README as inspiration only.
